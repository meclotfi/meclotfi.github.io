Channel-Remix

Efficient Task-Specific Knowledge Transfer via Pretrained Channel Remixing

Build a compact student by selecting and sparsely recombining pretrained channels, then fold those combinations into ordinary weights.

Read the paper ↗View method overview ↓
METHOD & EXPERIMENTS

The manuscript evaluates the method across 16 tasks and 7 pretraining families. Its main question is whether useful task-specific channels can be composed from pretrained directions with less calibration data.
