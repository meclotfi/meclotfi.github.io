01 / RESEARCH INTERESTS

Research focus areas.

I work on model design, adaptation, and deployment, mainly for vision tasks.

01 / FOUNDATION MODELS

Foundation models & adaptation

Adapting pretrained models to new tasks with meta-learning, adapters, and LoRA.

02 / COMPRESSION & EFFICIENCY

Model compression & efficient deep learning

Making models smaller and cheaper to run through pruning, channel recombination, and hardware-aware deployment.

03 / KNOWLEDGE TRANSFER

Knowledge distillation & meta-learning

Transferring knowledge from pretrained models and previous tasks to smaller task-specific models.

04 / AUTOML & NAS

AutoML & neural architecture search

Automating architecture and training choices under task and hardware constraints.
