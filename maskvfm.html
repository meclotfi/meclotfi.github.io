MaskVFM

Prompt-conditioned structured masking for vision encoders

Ongoing work on a hypernetwork that generates attention and FFN masks for SAM-style vision encoders from prompts and budget constraints.

Research status

MaskVFM explores the compression side of the thesis. A lightweight hypernetwork uses prompt and budget information to generate masks for attention and feed-forward computation.

This work is ongoing and is described in my thesis proposal.
