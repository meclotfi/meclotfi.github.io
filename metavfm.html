MetaVFM

Meta-Learned Efficient Adaptation of Vision Foundation Models for Medical Imaging

Use meta-learning to adapt foundation models to new medical imaging tasks with adapters and LoRA.

Read the paper ↗View method overview ↓Poster (PowerPoint) ↓
METHOD & EXPERIMENTS

A shared-weight Super-Adapter supports an adapter zoo built across 90+ datasets. The learned dataset–adapter meta-space retrieves configurations for unseen tasks; the framework is evaluated on 17 datasets.

2–3×faster convergence
under a fixed epoch budget
Reported in the manuscript; see the paper for evaluation settings.
